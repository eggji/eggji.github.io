About me

I am Jinlong Ji, a Member of Technical Staff at xAI (Palo Alto). I work on enterprise API post-training for Grok, including post-training for API models, small-model distillation, and evaluation for tool calling, long-context, and multimodal use.

Before that, I was a Senior Deep Learning Engineer on Discovery & Personalization at Roblox (ranking, recommendation, and related modeling). Earlier, I was a Software Engineer, Machine Learning on the Google Cloud AI Vision team in Pittsburgh, and I interned in deep learning research at Bosch Research.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from Case Western Reserve University (advisor: Prof. Pan Li), working on blockchain and privacy in distributed machine learning. I also studied at Mississippi State University and earlier at Xidian University (B.S. and M.S.).
