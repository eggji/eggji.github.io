About me

I am Jinlong Ji, a Member of Technical Staff at xAI, based in San Mateo. I work on post-training and last-mile engineering for LLM applications.

Before that, I was a Senior Deep Learning Engineer at Roblox. Earlier, I was a Software Engineer, Machine Learning at Google on the Google Cloud AI Vision team.

I received my PhD from Case Western Reserve University.
